# RELIASTRA research artifacts

Versioned, reproducible artifacts behind the papers published at
<https://reliastra.com/research>.

A paper is the argument. An artifact is the work. Neither substitutes for the
other, and a measurement that cannot be re-run is a claim rather than a result -
so anything RELIASTRA measures for publication lands here with its data, its
script and its reproduction steps.

## Conventions

Every artifact directory carries:

- `README.md` - problem, research question, method, environment, experiment,
  results, limitations, reproduction steps, license, references.
- The captured data, stored **verbatim**, with the read timestamp in the
  filename.
- A script that re-derives every published figure from that data. Derived
  datasets are generated by the script, never written by hand, so they cannot
  drift from the arithmetic.
- A license. Code is MIT; captured third-party material stays with its
  publisher.

Artifacts are written to be useful without the paper. If a reader finds the
directory first, the README should be enough to understand what was measured,
how, and what the result does and does not establish.

## Editorial standard

Nothing in this directory is synthetic, backfilled or estimated to fill a gap.
Where a figure could not be measured it is absent, and the paper says so. The
standard the corpus holds itself to is published at
<https://reliastra.com/research/reliastra-research-agenda>.
